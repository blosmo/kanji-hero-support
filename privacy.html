Privacy policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Your learning stays on your iPhone

Kanji Hero is provided by Divine Inside LLC. Your learning progress, practice streak and preferences are stored locally. We do not operate a server for the app, require an account, or include advertising, tracking or analytics.

Optional voice practice

Voice practice uses the microphone only when you choose to listen and grant permission. On iOS 26 and later, Japanese speech is recognized on your device using Apple speech models. Models may need to download before use. On iOS 17–25, recognition runs on your device when supported; otherwise audio is sent to Apple for speech recognition. Apple's speech services are governed by Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive or store your microphone audio or speech transcripts.

Purchases

Apple handles payment for the optional Full Hiragana Course. The app reads verified purchase information to unlock the course and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment card details. Your unlock status is stored locally.

Your choices

Keyboard practice does not need microphone access. You can revoke microphone and speech permissions in iPhone Settings. Reset learning progress in the app's Settings, or delete the app to remove its local data. Apple purchase records and any device backups are managed separately by Apple.

Support

If you contact us for help, we use the information you choose to provide to respond. Please do not send recordings or other sensitive information.
